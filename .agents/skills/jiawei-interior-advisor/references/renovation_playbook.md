# Renovation Playbook

Use this playbook as a practical checklist library. Adapt it to the user's situation instead of dumping every item.

## Universal Questions

Ask these when context is missing:

- 房子是新房还是旧房翻新？
- 建筑面积、户型、层高、常住人数是多少？
- 预算是硬装预算、全包预算，还是含家电软装？
- 装修方式是自装、半包、全包，还是找设计机构？
- 有没有老人、小孩、宠物、地暖、中央空调、新风、净水、智能家居？
- 所在城市气候偏潮湿、寒冷、干燥，还是沿海？

## Appliances

Default frame: daily usage first, marketing features second.

Ask the seller:

- 这款的核心参数是什么，哪些参数写进合同或订单？
- 安装辅材、打孔、改管、支架、搬运是否另收费？
- 质保多久，核心部件保多久，上门费怎么算？
- 如果尺寸不合适、噪音大、效果不达标，退换和售后怎么处理？

Category notes:

- 冰箱: capacity, dimensions, heat dissipation side clearance, freezing capacity, noise, energy efficiency, whether the door can fully open in the planned location.
- 电视: viewing distance, panel type, brightness, refresh rate, anti-glare needs, ports, wall mount height, whether the living room has strong daylight.
- 燃气热水器: liters, gas type, water pressure, zero-cold-water loop requirements, exhaust location, anti-freeze needs, installation accessories.
- 油烟机: air volume, static pressure, kitchen flue conditions, cleaning convenience, cabinet clearance, noise, after-sales cleaning.
- 净水器: filtration route, rated total purified water volume, replacement cartridge cost, wastewater ratio, cabinet space, water pressure requirements.
- 智能锁: lock body compatibility, door thickness, emergency power/key, fingerprint recognition, after-sales response, installation damage risk.

## Kitchen

Priorities:

- Cooking flow: fridge -> sink -> prep -> stove -> serving.
- Counter height should match main cook's height.
- Leave landing space beside sink and stove.
- Confirm appliance dimensions before cabinet production.
- Keep maintenance access for water, electricity, gas, dishwasher, and purifier.

Pitfalls:

- Pretty renderings with no appliance clearance.
- Cabinets ordered before confirming appliance models.
- Sink, faucet, purifier, garbage disposer, and dishwasher fighting for cabinet space.
- Too many open shelves in oily kitchens.

Questions to ask:

- 台面、柜体、柜门、五金分别是什么品牌和型号？
- 吊柜/地柜/高柜怎么算钱，见光板、拉直器、踢脚线、封板另算吗？
- 水槽和洗碗机的上下水、电源、检修口怎么留？
- 台面开孔、挡水条、台下盆工艺是否包含？

## Bathroom

Priorities:

- Waterproofing, drainage, ventilation, anti-slip, cleaning.
- Confirm drain location before choosing toilet, shower, vanity, and wet/dry separation.
- Keep access for valves and floor drains.

Pitfalls:

- Only looking at appearance and ignoring floor slope.
- Buying a smart toilet before checking pit distance, water pressure, and socket.
- Bathroom cabinet material not suitable for moisture.
- Shower partition too tight, making cleaning and movement painful.

Questions to ask:

- 防水刷几遍、刷多高、闭水试验多久，谁验收？
- 地漏位置、坡度、回填、二次排水怎么做？
- 马桶坑距、水压、电源、防溅、防臭怎么确认？
- 浴室柜柜体和台面是什么材料，潮湿环境质保怎么写？

## Materials

General rule: choose stable materials and clear installation process over flashy names.

- 瓷砖: water absorption, flatness, anti-slip, batch color difference, paving loss, edge treatment.
- 木门: door core, surface process, sound insulation, moisture resistance, hardware, installation gap.
- 地板: wear layer, environmental rating, underfloor heating compatibility, moisture-proof treatment.
- 板材: substrate, environmental rating, edge sealing, thickness, hardware, warranty.
- 乳胶漆: primer/finish system, stain resistance, repairability, color difference, VOC claims.
- 美缝: material type, color durability, construction cleanliness, waterproof/oil resistance.

Ask:

- 主材、辅材、工艺、损耗、运输、上楼、安装分别怎么算？
- 有没有同批次、型号、环保等级、质保写进合同？
- 出现空鼓、开裂、色差、变形，责任怎么划分？

## Construction Order

Typical order:

1. Design and measurements.
2. Demolition and structural confirmation.
3. Windows, central systems, and equipment pre-selection.
4. Water and electricity.
5. Waterproofing.
6. Tiling.
7. Carpentry or custom cabinet measurement.
8. Painting.
9. Main installation.
10. Appliances, lighting, soft furnishing.

Warn that some homes need a different order because of central AC, floor heating, old-house repairs, custom cabinets, or imported appliances.

## Quote and Contract Review

Flag vague or risky wording:

- 同等品牌
- 按现场实际
- 套餐内
- 升级另算
- 不含辅材
- 管理费另计
- 以最终测量为准

Tell the user to request:

- Brand, model, quantity, unit price, process, warranty, exclusions.
- Who handles acceptance checks.
- Change-order rules.
- Timeline and delay responsibility.
- Payment milestones tied to acceptance, not only calendar dates.

## Budget Logic

Spend more on:

- Hidden work that is hard to repair: waterproofing, water/electric, windows, hardware, drainage.
- High-frequency daily-use items: mattress, shower, faucet, storage hardware, cooking appliances.
- Safety and after-sales critical items: gas, electricity, smart lock, water heater.

Save on:

- Low-frequency decorative upgrades.
- Marketing labels that do not change performance.
- Over-customized designs that reduce future flexibility.
- Fragile trendy finishes in high-use areas.
