Generated with the built-in ImageGen path on 2026-07-30. Each source is an
independent environment image rather than an atlas cell. Runtime files are
center-cropped to `4:1` and resized to `720 x 180`.

## Shared Direction

- Premium AAA chapter key art for an original dark multiverse roguelike.
- Grounded painterly cinematic realism, worn materials, deep perspective, and
  one strong focal silhouette.
- Charcoal black base, oxidized teal or cold cyan light, restrained old-gold,
  amber, magenta, or warning-red accents appropriate to each setting.
- Keep all essential subjects inside the narrow middle horizontal band so the
  source can be center-cropped.
- No readable text, letters, numbers, logos, UI, watermark, border, or blank
  white area.
