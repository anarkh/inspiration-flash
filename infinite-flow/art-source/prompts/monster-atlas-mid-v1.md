# Monster Atlas Mid V1

- Grid: `4 x 3`, row-major, one full or three-quarter body monster per cell.
- Style: original realistic dark science-fantasy xuanhuan, unsettling but not gory, in the shared project palette.
- Exclude: text, logos, UI, frames, recognizable franchise costumes, merged creatures, and cross-cell overlap.

## Exact Order And Brief

1. `paper_librarian`: faceless custodian in layered black paper robes with blank folded pages orbiting as traps.
2. `dream_jailer`: broad masked keeper in restraint armor with teal dream-lock chains and an empty sealed cage.
3. `void_knight`: black plate knight whose fractured armor floats between cyan spatial seams.
4. `main_god_echo`: nonhuman projection of broken obsidian facets, aged-gold scan rings, and asymmetric copied limbs.
5. `clockwork_scout`: lean spring-legged mechanical hunter with one telescope lens and a cyan time trail.
6. `epoch_sentinel`: heavy automaton with era rings and an arm holding a luminous impact afterimage.
7. `zero_hour_regent`: abstract time sovereign with floating ring fragments and split past/future afterimages.
8. `verdict_usher`: swift masked tribunal enforcer with three interchangeable weapon seals.
9. `paradox_bailiff`: armored law enforcer whose torso and weapon occupy reversed causal positions.
10. `zero_sum_auditor`: monumental faceless adjudicator with two balance arms feeding an obsidian core.
11. `entropy_deckhand`: weathered ark crew revenant in pressure gear with crystallized disorder around its boots.
12. `dissipation_navigator`: skeletal navigation officer fused with a broken astrolabe harness and decaying route arcs.

Use consistent apparent scale, generous padding, and silhouettes that remain readable at `288 x 384`.
