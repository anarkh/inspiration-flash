# Modern And Future Content V1

Generated with the built-in ImageGen path on 2026-07-30. Every subject was
generated independently and then center-cropped or resized to the runtime
contract.

## Shared Direction

- Original, functional equipment and enemies for a dark multiverse roguelike.
- High-detail AAA inventory or bestiary concept art on a near-black charcoal
  background with a clear silhouette and generous safe padding.
- Modern assets use practical worn steel, polymer, olive-drab paint, and small
  amber or red indicators. Future assets use black titanium, ceramic coils,
  and restrained cyan phase light.
- No people, hands, readable text, letters, numbers, brands, logos, UI,
  watermark, border, or unrelated props.

## Equipment

1. `breach_shotgun` (`160 x 200`): contemporary compact pump-action breaching
   shotgun, short barrel, heat shield, shell carrier, worn matte-black steel
   and polymer, tiny amber status light; complete weapon in three-quarter view.
2. `phase_coil_rifle` (`160 x 200`): far-future shoulder-fired rifle built
   around an exposed electromagnetic accelerator spine, nested ceramic coils,
   compact power cell, angular stock, and thin cyan phase glow; complete weapon
   in three-quarter view.

## Monsters

1. `rogue_sentry` (`288 x 384`): plausible modern autonomous sentry turret on a
   rugged wheeled tripod, twin compact barrels, armored sensor head with a red
   aperture, exposed cables, chipped olive-drab and matte-black paint.
2. `phase_hunter_drone` (`288 x 384`): non-humanoid future aerial hunter with a
   manta-like black-titanium chassis, vector thrusters, central cyan phase lens,
   paired coil emitters, and contained positional afterimages.

The immutable runtime outputs live under `public/assets/equipment/` and
`public/assets/monsters/`; `src/game-assets.ts` owns the stable manifest keys.
